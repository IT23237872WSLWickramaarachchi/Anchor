# Anchor — User Guide

This guide walks you through using Anchor on your iPhone to save your parking spot, add garage details, navigate back, and manage historical sessions.

---

## 1. Saving Your Parking Spot

1. When you park your car, launch **Anchor**.
2. On the main **Parking** tab, tap the teal **Save parking** button.
3. The **Save Parking** screen will open.

### Choosing Your Location:
- **Automatic GPS**: Tap the **Use current location** button on the map. Anchor will perform a quick, one-shot GPS check and drop your pin at your current coordinates.
- **Manual Pin Selection**: If you are in an underground garage with weak GPS, or prefer to set the pin manually, simply **tap anywhere on the map**. A teal car pin will appear immediately. You can tap again to reposition the pin at any time.

---

## 2. Adding Floor, Bay, and Landmark Notes

While GPS marks the entrance or footprint of a parking structure, interior positioning requires manual notes:
1. **Location Name**: Enter a recognizable title (e.g., *"West Campus Garage"*, *"North Terminal Long Term"*). If left blank, Anchor automatically names it *"Parked car"*.
2. **Floor (Optional)**: Enter the garage level (e.g., *"Level 3"*, *"B2"*, *"Roof"*).
3. **Bay (Optional)**: Enter the bay, pillar, or row code (e.g., *"Bay 42B"*, *"Aisle 14"*).
4. **Notes (Optional)**: Enter landmarks to make finding your vehicle easy (e.g., *"Near the blue elevators by Door 4"*).
5. Tap **Save** in the top-right toolbar. The active parking card appears on your home screen.

---

## 3. Editing or Cancelling

- **Cancelling Before Save**: If you change your mind while creating a spot, tap **Cancel** in the top-left toolbar. No data is stored.
- **Editing an Active Spot**:
  1. On the **Parking** screen, tap **Edit parking**.
  2. The form loads your existing spot location and notes.
  3. Modify any field or adjust the map pin.
  4. Tap **Save** to update the session.

---

## 4. Returning to Your Car ("Walk to car")

1. Open **Anchor**. The map displays your car's exact pin with floor and bay callouts.
2. The card below shows how long ago you parked (e.g., *"Parked 45m ago"*).
3. Tap the primary teal button: **Walk to car**.
4. Anchor hands off your coordinates directly to **Apple Maps** with walking directions pre-selected.

---

## 5. Marking Your Car as Collected

1. Once you reach your vehicle, return to **Anchor**.
2. Tap **Collected car**.
3. A confirmation dialog appears asking: *"Have you returned to your car? This will complete your active session and move it to history."*
4. Tap **Mark as Collected**.
5. Anchor records the collection timestamp, calculates total duration, clears the active parking state, and moves the session to **History**.

---

## 6. Viewing and Deleting Parking History

1. Switch to the **History** tab using the bottom navigation bar.
2. Your completed sessions are displayed in reverse chronological order (newest first).
3. Each row shows:
   - Location title and floor/bay tags.
   - Date and time parked.
   - Total parked duration badge (e.g., *"2h 15m"*).
4. Tap any record to view its full details and a read-only map of where you parked.
5. **Deleting a Record**: Swipe left on any history row and tap **Delete**, then confirm the deletion dialog.

---

## 7. Troubleshooting Location Permissions

If you tapped "Don't Allow" when prompted for location access:
1. Open the iPhone **Settings** app.
2. Scroll down and tap **Anchor** (or **Privacy & Security > Location Services**).
3. Tap **Location** and choose **While Using the App**.
4. Return to Anchor.
*Note: You can always use Anchor without granting location permissions by tapping directly on the interactive map to place your pin manually.*
